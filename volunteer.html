Volunteer

Foggy Bluff is an all volunteer-run haunt benefiting non-profit Green Gables.

Want to help bring Foggy Bluff to life? We need scare actors, builders, guides, and more!

Volunteers age under 18 require parent/guardian permission to participate. Age 15 and below require a parent/guardian present on-property.

Questions? Reach out to us by email at Volunteer@FoggyBluff.com
