Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is it scary? Yes! Expect jump scares, fog, and eerie surprises. Major gore will be kept to a minimum, though there will be some present.
  • Is it kid-friendly? While this varies kid to kid, we recommend ages 10+ for our main night time show. For the younger kiddos, we will have a Trick'r'Treat walkthrough. Saturday 10/18 and 10/25 from 5pm-7pm.
  • Is it accessible? We do our best to accommodate everyone, though the Haunted Trail is outdoors on sometimes uneven grass and dirt. Contact us for specific questions and accomodations.
  • What if it rains? Light rain won't stop us! Severe weather may delay entry. If we close due to incliment weather, we will honor your ticket on a future night.
  • Where are you located? Foggy Bluff takes place on the grounds of Green Gables, located at 1501 S. Harbor City Blvd in Melbourne. Off US-1 just north of US-192.
  • Where should I park? Please park at the Reflections building, just north of Green Gables, located at 1499 S. Harbor City Blvd.
  • What is Green Gables? Green Gables is a nonprofit in Melbourne, FL with the goal to preserve local history. Head over to GreenGables.org for more information!
