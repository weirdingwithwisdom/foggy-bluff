About Us

The House on Foggy Bluff is a community-run haunted attraction hosted by the nonprofit Green Gables at Historic Riverview Village. Our mission is to bring spooky fun to the Space Coast while preserving local history. Each year, we create a new haunting theme—this year's is "Holiday Haunts," featuring twisted versions of your favorite celebrations.

We're running "Holiday Haunts" this year on Friday and Saturday nights through October.
